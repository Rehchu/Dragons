# Dragons

*The great dragon saga.* Target: 80,000 words.

A slow-burning epic of the Free Cities and the wyrms that were never merely beasts —
of a council that convened to judge a creature and found, too late, that the creature
had convened to judge them.

## The World, so far

- **The Free Cities** — a league of nine guild-and-warden magistracies, held together by
  the Long Cold's memory and by mutual debt more than by love.
- **The Hall of Nine Keys** — the seat of full council, opened only in gravest need. Built
  to hold judgment.
- **The Wyrms** — not beasts, not enemies, not kings. The old tongues had a word for them.
  The new law threw it away. In Chapter Three, a man says it again.
